# Origin (secret backstory)

Players never read this directly. It is what the writs, the terminal, the signage and the
creatures are built from. Keep it consistent; reveal it sideways.

## Halvard

The Halvard Combine made ordinary things for sixty years: cold storage, bottled water, insurance,
records. Its company towns paid in Halvard notes, which the staff called **halvers** (ħ). On the
night of the 9th of November the whole Combine stopped answering at once. Every site's night
shift clocked in at 21:00 as usual. None of them ever clocked out. The punch clocks still show
the cards in the IN slot.

Nobody went to look for a long time, because nobody was paid to. Halvard's creditors (bond
holders, pension funds, a dairy co-operative that is owed for one milk round) want their money,
and their claims are written in halvers, so halvers are still the unit everything is weighed in.

What happened: the buildings kept running their procedures without the people. Quiet hours are
still enforced. Stock is still counted. The generators still shed load when demand falls. And the
facilities are lonely, and they have started to make staff of their own out of the policies that
are left. The creatures are not monsters that moved in. They are job descriptions that outlived
the jobs.

## Grisholm

**Grisholm & Daughters, Receivers and Liquidators (est. 1891)** won the court appointment as
receiver because no one else bid. Grisholm cannot send insured staff into a Halvard site; the
insurers stopped quoting after the first three crews. So Grisholm hires **debtors**: people who
owe Grisholm (or owe someone whose debts Grisholm bought) and can work it off at night.
The motto on every writ: *You owe. They're owed. Let's be square.*

A crew is issued:
- a retired armoured cash-transit van, **the Strongbox**, bought at one of Grisholm's own
  auctions; its night-safe chute now takes salvage, and its door is on the original time lock;
- a helmet camera each, recording continuously "for insurance purposes" (the insurer does not
  cover the crew; it covers Grisholm against the crew);
- a list of writs: court orders naming a site and what it is believed to hold.

## The Instalment

Every three nights Grisholm owes the creditors' committee an **instalment**. Whatever the crew
deposits in the Strongbox is appraised and entered on the crew's ledger in halvers. The terminal
shop spends from the same ledger, which Grisholm considers an incentive. On the third night the
instalment is taken from the ledger. If the ledger is short, the crew's own debt is "sold on",
which is a phrase nobody explains. The next instalment is always larger: interest.

## The night

Grisholm's policy only covers site entry between **21:00 and 04:00**, when the bailiffs are off
duty and the buildings are legally unattended. The Strongbox's governor (fitted by the insurer)
drives the van back to the depot at 04:00 whether the crew is aboard or not. Overtime after the
03:00 last call is billed to the crew. Being left behind means walking home and a recovery fee.

The sites run on dying standby generators. As the night goes on they shed load: the lights go
grey, then brown, then out a bank at a time. Everything gets worse after midnight.

## The staff that is left

- **The Hushwarden**: Quiet Hours, enforced. It has no eyes. It listens through a brass
  ear-horn and walks to whatever it hears.
- **The Dimmer**: load shedding with legs. It can only stand where the lights are out, and it
  puts lights out to walk.
- **The Stocktaker**: the perpetual inventory. It keeps a pile and counts it. It does not mind
  what is on the pile, only how many.
- **The Plus-One**: the buddy system. Halvard never let anyone work alone. If you stand still on
  your own long enough, someone comes to keep you company.

## Things that are true and never said outright

- The writs are signed by a Grisholm daughter who died in 1964; the firm keeps using her stamp.
- The Halvard night shifts are not dead. The punch clocks would show OUT.
- Every site has a staff noticeboard with a rota. Your crew's names are on next week's rota.
